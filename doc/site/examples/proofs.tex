\section{Parse trees}\label{sec:trees}
\begin{definition}\label{def:tree}
A parse tree shows how a grammar derives a sentence.
\end{definition}
\begin{forest}
  for tree={draw, rounded corners}
  [S [NP [SaTeX]] [VP [V [reads]] [NP [the code]]]]
\end{forest}
\begin{lemma}\label{lem:leaves}
The leaves of a tree from Definition~\ref{def:tree}, read left to right, spell the sentence.
\end{lemma}
\begin{theorem}
Every sentence of the grammar has a tree, by Lemma~\ref{lem:leaves}.
\end{theorem}
As Section 1 shows, one tree is enough.
